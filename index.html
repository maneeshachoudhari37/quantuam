ABOUT PROJECT

Why Quantum Fraud Detection?

Credit-card fraud is a highly imbalanced classification problem. Most transactions are legitimate while fraudulent transactions are rare. Our proposed hybrid system combines classical preprocessing with quantum machine learning to identify suspicious transaction patterns.
